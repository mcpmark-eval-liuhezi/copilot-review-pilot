# Copilot Review Reading Checklist

A short guide for reading Copilot review feedback thoughtfully before acting on it.

- **Skim the whole review first.** Get the full picture of what Copilot flagged before responding to any single comment.
- **Never accept a suggestion you don't fully understand.** If you can't explain why a change is needed, investigate or ask a human before merging it in.
- **Check the affected lines yourself.** Open the diff in your editor and confirm the concern actually applies to your code, not just to a similar pattern elsewhere.
- **Treat it as a second opinion, not an authority.** Copilot can be confidently wrong — weigh its points against your own context and domain knowledge.
- **Note recurring patterns.** If the same category of issue appears across reviews, add it to your own pre-review checklist and catch it before Copilot does.
- **Close the loop on dismissals.** If you reject a suggestion, leave a one-line reason so reviewers (human and AI) learn what your team actually cares about.
